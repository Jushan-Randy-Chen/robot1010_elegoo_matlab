Textures for ElegooSimCar, the simulated room used by elegooMappingTemplate.m
(USE_SIMULATOR = true) and visualSlamDemo("sim").

floor_wood_planks, rug_carpet, wall_white_brick, wall_paint, wood_dark, wood_medium,
wood_light, marble, curtain, picture_meerkat, picture_lake, picture_stonehenge:
  Resized/cropped from the AWS RoboMaker Small House World
  https://github.com/aws-robotics/aws-robomaker-small-house-world (branch ros2)
  License: MIT No Attribution (see LICENSE_aws-robomaker-small-house-world.txt).
  The repository is archived (AWS RoboMaker reached end of support in 2025);
  the textures are used as plain images only.

Book spines on the bookshelf are cut at run time from the book-cover photos that
ship with the Computer Vision Toolbox (toolbox/vision/visiondata/bookCovers).
Cardboard boxes, the PC tower, door, window, TV and outlets are drawn in code.
